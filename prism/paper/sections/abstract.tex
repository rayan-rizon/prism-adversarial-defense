We present \prism (Predictive Runtime Immune System with Manifold Monitoring), 
a multi-backbone adversarial-input detection system that detects adversarial 
examples at inference time using topological data analysis (TDA) of internal 
neural network activations. \prism integrates four complementary components: 
(1)~\tamm (Topological Activation Manifold Monitor), which extracts persistence 
diagrams from per-layer activations and scores each inference against a 
calibrated clean-data profile using Wasserstein distance; 
(2)~\cadg (Conformal Adversarial Detection Guarantee), which provides 
distribution-free false-positive rate certificates at three severity tiers via 
split conformal prediction; 
(3)~\sacd (Sequential Adversarial Campaign Detector), a CUSUM-on-score-stream
monitor that flags sustained attack campaigns at a mean latency of
\textbf{2.6 queries} on $\rho{=}1.0$ streams while maintaining $0.0$
false-alarm activation on clean-only streams up to $1{,}000$ queries
(longer streams need a more conservative threshold $C^*{\geq}8$ or
periodic reset; App.~\ref{app:l0_stress}); and
(4)~\tamsh (Topology-Aware MoE Self-Healing), which recovers correct
predictions on L3-rejected adversarials through attack-specialist expert
sub-networks.

The lightweight topology gate (combined $H_0{+}H_1$
Wasserstein) delivers \textbf{+10.6pp} recovery gap over passthrough under
PGD, short of a pre-registered $+15$~pp target; a \emph{learned} router
trained on the L3 pool (logistic regression on final-layer activations)
clears it, recovering \textbf{28.7\%} of L3-rejected PGD inputs --
$9.1\times$ a uniform $1/K$ control ($3.2\%$, which isolates the routing
signal from expert capacity) and matching a prior-augmented upper bound
($29.3\%$, which hardcodes the PGD specialist) \emph{without} that
hardcoding (App.~\ref{app:tamsh_diag}).

We evaluate \prism on three full CNN settings (5 seeds,
$n{=}1000$/seed, de-duplicated per Table~\ref{tab:main_multi}):
(i) CIFAR-10 with a
CIFAR-native ResNet-18 backbone, (ii) CIFAR-10 with a WRN-28-10
backbone, and (iii) CIFAR-100 with a ResNet-18 backbone, under
standard attacks (FGSM, PGD, CW-L2, Square, and AutoAttack where run),
with per-channel
ablation, design-FPR ($10\%$) baseline comparison against LID,
Mahalanobis, ODIN, and Energy (matched on CIFAR-10/ResNet-18; three
baselines exceed the $10\%$ target on WRN-28-10/CIFAR-100, reported
unfiltered), campaign detection, and L3 recovery. We additionally run a
standard-attack transformer transfer check on CIFAR-10/ViT-B/16
(FGSM, PGD-50, and Square only). On the main CIFAR-10/ResNet-18 setting,
\prism Full achieves
$0.918$ mean TPR on FGSM/PGD/Square (matched empirical FPR $0.074$),
$0.938$ on CW-L2 ($0.075$), and $1.000$ on the AutoAttack ensemble's
final adversarials ($0.075$; individual sub-attacks detected at
$0.71$--$0.97$ @ L1, weakest standalone Square $0.706$,
App.~\ref{app:autoattack}), beating
the strongest reproduced baseline (Mahalanobis $0.582$ mean TPR at FPR
$0.077$) by $+33.6$~pp on the FGSM/PGD/Square pool. On the
CIFAR-10/ResNet-18 setting, an ensemble-complete adaptive PGD surrogate
drops all-input TPR to
$0.479$, but \prism still detects $0.866$ of successful adversarials
and leaves a $0.074$ undetected-success rate. The WRN-28-10
backbone lifts the same pool to $0.975$ at FPR $0.073$; CIFAR-100 holds
$0.830$ at FPR $0.077$; ViT-B/16 reaches
$1.000/1.000/0.9998$ TPR on FGSM/PGD-50/Square at FPR $0.0828$
(no ViT latency or adaptive-attack claim). Per-setting tables and the
full CIFAR-100 appendix are reported in \S\ref{sec:multi_backbone} and
Appendix~\ref{app:cifar100}; WRN/CIFAR-100 adaptive-PGD probes remain
appendix-only stress evidence, not ensemble-complete headline claims.
Unlike detector-coupled defenses, \prism operates on any wrappable
pre-trained classifier with accessible intermediate activations---
requiring per-model clean profiling and conformal calibration but no
backbone retraining or architecture modification---and is, to
our knowledge, the first activation-based adversarial detector to provide
a split-conformal, distribution-free false-positive-rate certificate.
